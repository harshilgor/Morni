MORNI HOMEPAGE REVENUE TODO
============================

Timing note
-----------
Start these experiments after enough vendors have uploaded enough products to
make recommendations representative and useful. Before that point, prioritize
catalog quality, accurate fabric/material data, imagery, and vendor adoption.

[ ] Complete the look
    Recommend complementary products and coordinated outfits from the same or
    nearby categories. Start on product detail pages, then add selected
    homepage placements once recommendation quality is reliable.

[ ] Limited-stock and selling-fast signals
    Show honest inventory and velocity labels such as "Only 2 left" or
    "Selling fast" when thresholds are supported by real catalog/order data.

[ ] Stronger For You personalization
    Use browsing, recently viewed, wishlist, category, price, and purchase
    signals to improve the For You rail. Measure click-through, add-to-bag,
    and conversion lift against a non-personalized baseline.

[ ] Store-specific bundles
    Let vendors promote coordinated sets, outfit bundles, or multi-item offers
    with clear pricing and inventory rules.

[ ] Outfit and accessory recommendations
    Add relevant complementary pieces such as jewellery, bags, shoes, or
    layering items without overwhelming the primary product journey.

[ ] Delivery urgency messaging
    Show accurate delivery expectations and, where supported, messages such as
    "Order now for today's delivery." Never create urgency without live
    availability and delivery data.

[ ] Homepage merchandising analytics
    Track impressions, rail/tab clicks, product opens, add-to-bag, checkout,
    and purchases by homepage section. Use this to test ordering, copy, and
    card density.

[ ] Vendor-funded featured placements
    Explore paid or sponsored visibility for vendors only after measurement,
    disclosure, eligibility, and ranking safeguards are defined.

Success criteria
---------------
For each item, define a control group and monitor revenue per session,
add-to-bag rate, checkout conversion, average order value, repeat visits, and
vendor participation. Keep recommendation and merchandising changes reversible.
